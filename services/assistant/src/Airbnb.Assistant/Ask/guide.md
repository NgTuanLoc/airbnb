How the site works:

- Booking: on a place's page pick dates and guests, press Reserve, then Confirm and pay with a card. The card is charged when you book. Stays are 1 to 30 nights, check-in can't be in the past, and each place has a guest limit.
- Price: price per night times the nights, plus a $75 cleaning fee per stay, plus a service fee of 14% of the nightly total.
- Test cards (no real money is charged): 4242 4242 4242 4242 succeeds; 4000 0000 0000 0002 is declined; 4000 0000 0000 9995 has insufficient funds; 4000 0000 0000 0077 is slow, so the trip shows "Payment processing" for a minute or two before it's confirmed. Any future expiry and any CVC work.
- Declined payment: the nights are freed and the trip shows "Payment declined". Book again with another card.
- Cancelling: go to Trips, open the trip and press Cancel, any time before the check-in day. A paid booking is refunded; a booking whose payment was still processing is voided. A trip that has started can't be cancelled.
- Emails: you get an email when a booking is confirmed or cancelled, and a reminder to review the place the morning after check-out.
- Messaging a host: press Message host on a place's page; conversations are under Messages. The site's original places have demo hosts who send one automatic reply.
- Hosting: any signed-in person can list a place from the Host dashboard (New listing: photos, price, guests, amenities). Hosts can't book their own place, and an unlisted place doesn't take bookings.
- Wishlists: press the heart on a place to save it to a wishlist.
- Accounts: sign up with an email and password, or continue with Google, GitHub or single sign-on for work emails. Login & security in the account menu shows connected accounts.
